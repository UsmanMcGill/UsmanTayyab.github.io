About me

I am an information systems researcher with a background spanning engineering, public service and management research. I was a first generation university graduate and the first in my family to study abroad.

I studied Mechanical Engineering at the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore, then joined the State Bank of Pakistan after a competitive national examination. A Chinese Government Scholarship supported a return to higher education. I subsequently pursued doctoral research in Management, concentrating in Information Systems, at McGill University's Desautels Faculty of Management.

My research examines corporate engagement with social and environmental issues on digital platforms, bringing together organizational theory, computational social science and digital trace methods.

Research interests

My work connects corporate communication, activism and sustainability with computational approaches to the study of digital public discourse.
